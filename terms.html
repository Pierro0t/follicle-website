2. Description of the App

Follicle is an app for tracking hair loss. From your photos and answers, an AI (OpenAI) gives ratings from which the app estimates a score, shows how it changes over time and offers general tips.

Important: Follicle is for informational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnoses. Always consult a qualified dermatologist or healthcare professional for medical advice.
